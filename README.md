# Resume to Portfolio

An open-source platform that helps job seekers turn their experience into a professional online presence. Built by developers, for those seeking their next opportunity.

The goal is to make essential portfolio-building tools free and accessible, while giving students and developers opportunities to contribute to a real-world project.

## 🌟 Why this project?

Creating a polished portfolio can take time, technical knowledge, and money. Job seekers often have to repeat the same information across resumes and websites, then update each one whenever their experience changes.

This project aims to make that process easier for fresh graduates, unemployed individuals, career changers, and anyone looking for their next opportunity. The essential portfolio-building features are intended to remain free.

## 🚀 How it is intended to work

1. **📄 Upload your resume:** Start with an existing PDF or DOCX. AI-assisted extraction creates a structured profile for you to review and correct.
2. **✏️ Make it yours:** Edit your details, skills, experience, education, and projects, and choose a portfolio template.
3. **🔗 Share your portfolio:** Publish a personal link that recruiters and employers can visit.
4. **🔄 Update once:** Edit your profile when you learn a skill or complete a project. Your published portfolio is intended to reflect the changes without requiring you to edit code or redeploy the site.
5. **📥 Export a resume:** Generate an updated PDF resume from the same profile when you need one.

## 💡 How it can help

- **Save time:** Start from a resume instead of building a portfolio from scratch.
- **Stay consistent:** Keep your professional information in one structured profile for both your website and resume.
- **Share your work:** Give recruiters a link to your projects, skills, and experience.
- **Keep it current:** Update your information through the platform rather than maintaining and deploying a personal site yourself.
- **Build experience together:** Contributors can practice development, design, documentation, and collaboration on a project with a social purpose.

## 🛠️ Planned features

- Upload a PDF or DOCX resume and use AI-assisted extraction to create a structured profile.
- Edit personal details, experience, skills, education, and projects in one place.
- Publish a portfolio at a shareable public URL and choose from different templates.
- Export the updated profile as a PDF resume.
- Control profile visibility and manage access through user accounts.
- Add an AI-powered digital twin that answers questions using the owner's approved professional information.

## 🗺️ Development phases

1. **MVP:** Resume upload and extraction, profile editing, public portfolios, and PDF export.
2. **Digital twin:** Grounded AI chat, more templates, and customization.
3. **Community and growth:** Community-led improvements, possible custom domains, and scalability enhancements.

## 💻 Proposed technology

The proposed stack is Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL and Auth, an LLM API, and Vercel. PostgreSQL with JSONB can hold the structured profile; pgvector may support retrieval for the digital twin later.

## 🤝 Contributing

Contributions to features, templates, fixes, and documentation are welcome. Contribution guidelines and setup instructions will be added as the project takes shape.

This project is in the planning stage; the features and technology choices above are proposed, not yet implemented.
